# The Farewell Hajj

## s01 -- The Announcement
Image: desert/dunes-day.svg | pan-right | Empty desert dunes under a pale sky. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

In the tenth year after the Hijrah, it was announced that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ would make the pilgrimage. <!-- c34-001 -->

His Companion Jabir said that a great many people came to Madinah, every one of them wishing to follow him, and to do as he did. <!-- c34-001 -->

Jabir said: "I looked, as far as my eye could reach, in front of him: riders, and people on foot.
And on his right, the same. And on his left, the same. And behind him, the same." <!-- c34-001 -->

It was the only pilgrimage he made after the Hijrah. <!-- c34-002 -->
As he performed its rites, he said to them:
"Learn your rites from me.
For I do not know whether I shall make the pilgrimage again, after this one." <!-- c34-003 -->

## s02 -- At Arafah
Image: mountains/ridges-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | A wide plain below layered ridges. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

On the ninth day of the month of pilgrimage, he came to the plain of Arafah.
When the sun had passed its height, he rode down into the valley, and addressed the people from his camel. <!-- c34-004 -->

"Your blood and your property are sacred to one another, as sacred as this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this land of yours." <!-- c34-004 -->

"Everything of the days of ignorance is beneath my feet, abolished.
The blood-feuds of the days of ignorance are abolished.
And the usury of the days of ignorance is abolished.
The first usury that I abolish is our own: that of al-Abbas, the son of Abd al-Muttalib.
All of it is abolished." <!-- c34-004 -->

"Fear Allah in your treatment of women.
You have taken them as a trust from Allah.
They have rights over you: that you provide for them, and clothe them, in fairness." <!-- c34-004 -->

"I have left among you that which, if you hold fast to it, you will never go astray: the Book of Allah." <!-- c34-004 -->

## s03 -- O Allah, Bear Witness
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide open sky. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Then he said to them:
"You will be asked about me.
So what will you say?" <!-- c34-005 -->

They said: "We bear witness that you have conveyed the message, and fulfilled your trust, and given sincere counsel." <!-- c34-005 -->

He raised his forefinger toward the sky, then pointed it toward the people.
"O Allah, bear witness.
O Allah, bear witness.
O Allah, bear witness." <!-- c34-005 -->

## s04 -- This Day
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a valley ringed by mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

It was a Friday, and he was standing at Arafah, when a verse was revealed. <!-- c34-006 -->
Its meaning is:
"This day I have perfected your religion for you,
and completed My favour upon you,
and chosen for you Islam as your religion." <!-- c34-007 -->

Years later, a Jewish man said to Umar that if such a verse had been revealed to his people, they would have made that day a festival.
Umar said: "I know the day on which it was revealed, and the place.
It was revealed to the Prophet while he was standing at Arafah, on a Friday." <!-- c34-006 -->

## s05 -- The Day of Sacrifice
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | slow-zoom | A valley beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The next day, at Mina, he spoke to them again. <!-- c34-008 -->

"What day is this?"
His Companion Abu Bakrah said: "We were silent, until we thought he would give it another name."
He said: "Is it not the day of sacrifice?"
They said: "Yes." <!-- c34-008 -->

"What month is this? Is it not the month of pilgrimage?"
"Yes."
"What land is this? Is it not the sacred land?"
"Yes." <!-- c34-008 -->

He said: "Then your blood, your property, and your honour are sacred to one another,
as sacred as this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this land of yours,
until the day you meet your Lord." <!-- c34-008 -->

"Have I conveyed?"
They said: "Yes."
He said: "O Allah, bear witness.
Let the one who is present convey it to the one who is absent.
For it may be that the one to whom it is conveyed will understand it better than the one who heard it." <!-- c34-008 -->

"Do not go back, after I am gone, to being unbelievers, striking one another's necks." <!-- c34-008 -->

## s06 -- Farewell
Image: desert/dunes-night.svg | slow-zoom | Desert dunes at night under a thin moon. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

His Companion Ibn Umar said: "We used to speak of the Farewell Pilgrimage, while the Prophet was still among us.
And we did not know what the farewell meant." <!-- c34-009 -->

At Arafah, he asked the listeners to bear witness that he had delivered the message. <!-- c34-005 -->

Then he returned to Madinah. <!-- c34-002 -->
