# The Year of Sorrow

## s01 -- After the Ravine
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a valley town ringed by mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The clans had come out of the ravine.
It was the tenth year of the mission. <!-- c14-001 -->

Within a short time, the historians report, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ lost the two people who had stood closest to him:
the uncle who had protected him, and the wife who had believed in him first. <!-- c14-001 -->

## s02 -- Abu Talib
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-left | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Abu Talib had raised him from the age of eight.
For ten years, he had stood between his nephew and Quraysh. <!-- c14-002 -->

When death came to Abu Talib, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ went in to him.
He found Abu Jahl sitting beside the bed, and with him another of the leaders of Quraysh. <!-- c14-003 -->

He said: "Uncle, say: There is no god but Allah.
One word, with which I can plead for you before Allah." <!-- c14-003 -->

The two men said: "Abu Talib, will you turn away from the religion of Abd al-Muttalib?"
He kept offering it to him, and they kept repeating what they had said.
Until the last thing Abu Talib said to them was that he remained on the religion of Abd al-Muttalib. <!-- c14-003 -->

The Messenger of Allah said: "I will go on asking forgiveness for you, until I am forbidden to." <!-- c14-003 -->

## s03 -- You Do Not Guide Whom You Love
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Then a verse was revealed.
Its meaning is:
"It is not for the Prophet, and those who believe, to ask forgiveness for those who set up partners with Allah, even if they are near of kin." <!-- c14-004 -->

And another verse was revealed about Abu Talib.
Its meaning is:
"You do not guide whom you love. But Allah guides whom He wills." <!-- c14-005 -->

He had urged Abu Talib to say the words of faith. The Qur'an said that guidance was not his to give. <!-- c14-003 c14-005 -->

## s04 -- Khadijah
Image: desert/oasis-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | Palm trees at an oasis at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Khadijah, may Allah be pleased with her, died three years before he left for Madinah. <!-- c14-006 -->

The historians report that her death came soon after that of Abu Talib, in the same year.
Which of them died first, and how many days lay between them, is reported differently. <!-- c14-007 -->

She had been his wife for twenty-five years. <!-- c14-008 -->
She had covered him when he came down from Hira, and told him that Allah would never disgrace him. <!-- c14-009 -->

He did not forget her.
Years later, when his wife Aishah spoke of how often he mentioned Khadijah, he said:
"I was given the gift of love for her." <!-- c14-010 -->

## s05 -- A Name for the Year
Image: desert/dunes-night.svg | slow-zoom | Desert dunes at night under a thin moon. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Later writers gave this year a name: the Year of Sorrow.
The name is theirs. It is not established as his own word for it. <!-- c14-011 -->

But what the year meant for him is not in doubt.
With Abu Talib gone, the historians report, Quraysh dared to do to him what they had not dared before. <!-- c14-012 -->

They report that a man threw dust on his head in the street.
He went home, and one of his daughters washed it away, weeping.
And it is reported that he said to her: "Do not weep, my daughter. Allah will protect your father." <!-- c14-012 -->

Makkah was closing to him.
So he looked beyond it, to a town in the hills to the east. <!-- c14-012 -->
