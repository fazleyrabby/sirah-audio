# His Legacy

## s01 -- What He Did Not Leave
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-left | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The reports describe what he left behind. <!-- n -->

His brother-in-law said:
"When the Messenger of Allah died, he left neither a dirham nor a dinar, nor a slave, nor anything else,
except his white mule, his weapons, and a piece of land that he had given away as charity." <!-- c37-001 -->

Aishah said: "The Messenger of Allah died while his coat of mail was held in pledge, for thirty measures of barley." <!-- c37-002 -->

Even that little was not passed on to his family.
He had said: "We are not inherited from.
What we leave behind is charity." <!-- c37-003 -->

## s02 -- His Character
Image: desert/oasis-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | Palm trees at an oasis at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

A man once came to Aishah, and asked her to describe the character of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.
She said: "Do you not read the Qur'an?"
He said: "I do."
She said: "The character of the Prophet of Allah was the Qur'an." <!-- c37-004 -->

The Qur'an itself says of him:
"You are truly of a tremendous character." <!-- c37-005 -->

Anas entered his household as a boy, and served him until his death.
He said: "I served the Prophet for ten years.
He never once said to me: Uff.
He never said, of something I had done: Why did you do that?
Nor of something I had left undone: Why did you not do it?" <!-- c37-006 -->

And he himself said:
"The best of you is the one who is best to his family.
And I am the best of you to my family." <!-- c37-007 -->

## s03 -- A Mercy
Image: desert/dunes-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | Empty desert at dawn, light spreading over the dunes. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The Qur'an gives the purpose of his mission in a single verse.
Its meaning is:
"We have not sent you except as a mercy to all the worlds." <!-- c37-008 -->

We have seen what that meant in the course of his life.
At Ta'if, when he was offered the destruction of those who had stoned him, and asked instead for their children. <!-- c37-009 -->
In Makkah, when the people who had driven him out stood before him, and he gave them safety. <!-- c37-010 -->
At Hunayn, when he sent the families of his enemies home. <!-- c37-011 -->

## s04 -- What He Left
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

What he left was not wealth. <!-- c37-001 c37-002 -->

He left the Qur'an.
At Arafah he had said: "I have left among you that which, if you hold fast to it, you will never go astray: the Book of Allah." <!-- c37-012 -->

In his lifetime it was written down by his scribes, and carried in the memory of his Companions.
Soon after his death, under Abu Bakr, it was gathered into a single collection. <!-- c37-013 -->

And he left his own example.
The Qur'an says:
"In the Messenger of Allah you have an excellent example, for whoever hopes in Allah and the Last Day, and remembers Allah often." <!-- c37-014 -->

He had asked those who heard him to carry it onward.
"Convey from me, even if it is a single verse." <!-- c37-015 -->
