# The Pledges of Aqabah

## s01 -- Who Will Take Me to His People
Image: desert/dunes-day.svg | pan-right | Empty desert dunes under a pale sky. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Each year, in the season of pilgrimage, the tribes of Arabia came to Makkah.
And each year, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ went out to meet them. <!-- c17-001 -->

His Companion Jabir narrated that he would present himself to the people at the gathering places, saying:
"Is there any man who will take me to his people?
For Quraysh have prevented me from conveying the word of my Lord." <!-- c17-002 -->

The historians of the Seerah report that he went from one tribe's camp to the next,
and that his uncle Abu Lahab would follow behind him, telling the people not to listen. <!-- c17-001 -->

## s02 -- Six Men from Yathrib
Image: maps/arabia.svg | static | A map of the Arabian peninsula with Makkah and Yathrib marked. | 0 | 24% 50%
Audio: Voice only.

Some two hundred and fifty miles north of Makkah lay the oasis of Yathrib.
Two Arab tribes lived there, the Aws and the Khazraj, alongside several Jewish tribes. <!-- c17-003 -->

The Aws and the Khazraj had been at war with each other for years.
Their last great battle was called Bu'ath.
Aishah said of it: "The day of Bu'ath was a day that Allah brought about for His Messenger.
When he came to them, they were divided, and their leaders had been killed." <!-- c17-004 -->

In the eleventh year of the mission, the historians report, he met six men of the Khazraj at a place called Aqabah, near Mina.
He sat with them, called them to Allah, and recited the Qur'an to them. <!-- c17-005 -->

They had heard their Jewish neighbours speak of a prophet whose time was near.
The historians report that they said to one another: "This is the prophet they warned you of. Do not let them reach him before you."
They believed, and they went home. <!-- c17-005 -->

## s03 -- The First Pledge
Image: mountains/ridges-dusk.svg | pan-left | Layered mountain ridges at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The next year, twelve men came. <!-- c17-006 -->

One of them, Ubadah ibn as-Samit, narrated what they pledged at Aqabah.
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to them:
"Pledge to me that you will not set up anything beside Allah.
That you will not steal.
That you will not commit adultery.
That you will not kill your children.
That you will not bring a slander you have invented.
And that you will not disobey me in what is right." <!-- c17-007 -->

"Whoever among you keeps it, his reward is with Allah."
And Ubadah said: "So we pledged to him on that." <!-- c17-007 -->

## s04 -- Mus'ab
Image: desert/oasis-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | Palm trees at an oasis at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

When they returned to Yathrib, he sent a teacher with them. <!-- c17-008 -->

Al-Bara, a man of Yathrib, later said:
"The first to come to us were Mus'ab ibn Umayr and Ibn Umm Maktum.
They taught the people the Qur'an." <!-- c17-008 -->

The historians report that Mus'ab stayed in the house of As'ad ibn Zurarah, and went from clan to clan,
and that through him two of the leaders of the Aws, Usayd ibn Hudayr and Sa'd ibn Mu'adh, accepted Islam,
and their people after them. <!-- c17-009 -->

Within a year, they report, there was hardly a house in Yathrib in which Islam was not spoken of. <!-- c17-009 -->

## s05 -- The Second Pledge
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

In the thirteenth year of the mission, the pilgrims of Yathrib came again.
Among them, the historians report, were seventy-three men and two women who had come for one purpose. <!-- c17-010 -->

They slipped away from their camp in the last third of the night, in ones and twos, and gathered at Aqabah. <!-- c17-010 -->

Jabir narrated what they asked him, and what he answered.
They said: "Messenger of Allah, on what shall we pledge to you?"
He said: "That you will hear and obey, in energy and in weariness.
That you will spend, in hardship and in ease.
That you will enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong.
That you will speak for Allah, without fearing the blame of anyone.
And that you will support me when I come to you, and protect me as you protect yourselves, your wives, and your children.
And for you is Paradise." <!-- c17-011 -->

They rose, and gave him their hands. <!-- c17-011 -->

## s06 -- Better Than Badr
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a valley town ringed by mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Ka'b ibn Malik was one of those who were there that night. <!-- c17-012 -->

Long afterwards, he said:
"I was present with the Messenger of Allah on the night of Aqabah, when we pledged ourselves to Islam.
I would not exchange it for Badr, even though Badr is more spoken of among the people." <!-- c17-012 -->

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ now had what he had sought for years, in the camps of the tribes:
a people who would take him in.
And his Companions in Makkah had somewhere to go. <!-- c17-010 c17-011 -->
