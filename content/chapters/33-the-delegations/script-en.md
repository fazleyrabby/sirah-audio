# The Delegations

## s01 -- The Arabs Had Been Waiting
Image: maps/arabia.svg | static | A map of the Arabian peninsula with Makkah, Madinah, Najran and Yemen marked.
Audio: Voice only.

Amr ibn Salamah said the Arab tribes had waited to see the result in Makkah before accepting Islam. <!-- c33-001 -->

A man of one of those tribes, Amr ibn Salamah, described it.
He said: "The Arabs were holding back from Islam until the conquest.
They would say: Leave him and his people.
If he prevails over them, then he is a true prophet." <!-- c33-001 -->

"So when Makkah was opened, every tribe hurried to declare its Islam.
And my father hurried to do so on behalf of our people." <!-- c33-001 -->

The ninth year after the Hijrah became known as the Year of Delegations. <!-- c33-002 -->

## s02 -- Those Who Came
Image: desert/oasis-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | Palm trees at an oasis at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

They came to Madinah from every part of the peninsula. <!-- c33-002 -->

One man described what his people had been before.
"We used to worship a stone.
And when we found a better stone, we would throw the first away, and take the other." <!-- c33-003 -->

When the delegation of the tribe of Abd al-Qays arrived, from the far east of Arabia, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ greeted them:
"Welcome to the people who have come neither in disgrace, nor in regret." <!-- c33-004 -->

They said that they could reach him only in the sacred months, because hostile tribes lay between them.
So they asked him for a few clear instructions that they could carry home.
He told them to believe in Allah alone, to establish the prayer, to give the alms, and to fast in Ramadan. <!-- c33-004 -->

## s03 -- Thumamah
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-left | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Not everyone came willingly. <!-- c33-005 -->

Abu Hurayrah told of a chief of the tribe of Hanifah, named Thumamah, who was captured by a patrol and brought to Madinah.
He was tied to a pillar in the mosque. <!-- c33-005 -->

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ came to him, and asked: "What do you have to say, Thumamah?"
He answered: "I have good to say, Muhammad.
If you kill me, you kill a man whose blood will be avenged.
If you show favour, you show it to one who is grateful.
And if you want wealth, ask, and you will be given what you wish." <!-- c33-005 -->

He left him.
The next day he asked again, and received the same answer.
And the third day, the same.
Then he said: "Release Thumamah." <!-- c33-005 -->

Thumamah went to a garden near the mosque, washed, came back in, and declared that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.
Then he said: "By Allah, Muhammad, there was no face on earth more hateful to me than yours.
And now your face is the dearest of all faces to me." <!-- c33-005 -->

## s04 -- The Christians of Najran
Image: mountains/ridges-dusk.svg | pan-right | Layered mountain ridges at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

From the south came a delegation of the Christians of Najran, led by two of their chiefs. <!-- c33-006 -->

They disputed with him about Isa, the son of Maryam.
The Qur'an invited both sides to call down the curse of Allah upon whichever of them was lying. <!-- c33-007 -->

His Companion Hudhayfah narrated that one of the two chiefs said to the other: "Do not do it.
By Allah, if he is a prophet, and we do this, neither we nor our descendants after us will prosper." <!-- c33-006 -->

So they came to terms with him instead, and kept their religion.
And they said: "Send with us a man who is trustworthy."
He said: "I will send with you a trustworthy man, truly trustworthy."
And he sent Abu Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah. <!-- c33-006 -->

## s05 -- The House Made Pure
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a valley town ringed by mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

In that same year, he sent Abu Bakr to lead the pilgrimage. <!-- c33-008 -->

Abu Hurayrah said: "Abu Bakr sent me, with others, to announce to the people on the day of sacrifice:
After this year, no idolater shall make the pilgrimage.
And no one shall go around the House unclothed." <!-- c33-008 -->

And he sent teachers and judges out to the lands that had entered Islam.
When he sent Mu'adh ibn Jabal to Yemen, he told him:
"You are going to a people of the Book.
Call them first to bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that I am the Messenger of Allah.
If they accept that, tell them that Allah has enjoined on them five prayers in every day and night.
If they accept that, tell them that Allah has enjoined on them alms, taken from their rich and given to their poor.
And beware of the prayer of one who has been wronged.
For between it and Allah, there is no veil." <!-- c33-009 -->
