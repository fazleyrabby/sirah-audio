# The Passing of the Messenger ﷺ

## s01 -- The Highest Companion
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | slow-zoom | Flat-roofed houses with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Aishah, may Allah be pleased with her, was with him at the end.
What we know of those last moments, we know from her. <!-- c36-001 c36-002 -->

She said that when he was in health, he used to say:
"No prophet is taken until he has been shown his place in Paradise, and then is given the choice." <!-- c36-001 -->

Her brother came into the room, holding a tooth-stick.
She saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ looking at it, and knew that he wanted it.
She took it, softened it for him, and he cleaned his teeth with it. <!-- c36-002 -->

There was a vessel of water beside him.
He would put his hands into the water, and wipe his face, and say:
"There is no god but Allah. Truly, death has its pangs." <!-- c36-002 -->

Then, with his head resting against her, he raised his eyes toward the ceiling, and said:
"O Allah. With the highest companion." <!-- c36-001 -->

Aishah said: "I said to myself: Then he will not choose us.
And I knew that this was what he used to tell us." <!-- c36-001 -->

## s02 -- He Was Sixty-Three
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Those were the last words he spoke. <!-- c36-001 -->

Aishah said: "It was one of the favours of Allah upon me that the Messenger of Allah died in my house, on my day, resting against my chest." <!-- c36-002 -->

It was a Monday, in the month of Rabi' al-Awwal, in the eleventh year after the Hijrah. <!-- c36-003 -->
He was sixty-three years old. <!-- c36-004 -->

His daughter Fatimah said:
"O my father, who answered the call of his Lord.
O my father, whose home is the Garden of Paradise.
O my father, to Jibril we give the news of his death." <!-- c36-005 -->

## s03 -- The People Could Not Believe It
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | pan-right | A town at night beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The news went out into the mosque, and the people were stunned. <!-- c36-006 -->

Umar stood up and said: "By Allah, the Messenger of Allah has not died."
He said afterwards: "By Allah, nothing else would enter my heart." <!-- c36-006 -->

Abu Bakr had been at his house on the edge of Madinah.
He came on his horse, dismounted, and entered the mosque.
He spoke to no one.
He went straight in to Aishah, and to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, who lay covered with a striped cloak. <!-- c36-007 -->

He uncovered his face, bent over him, and kissed him, and wept.
He said: "May my father and my mother be your ransom.
You were good in life, and you are good in death." <!-- c36-007 -->

## s04 -- Allah Is Living
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a town among mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Then Abu Bakr went out to the people.
Umar was still speaking.
Abu Bakr told him to sit, and the people turned to Abu Bakr. <!-- c36-008 -->

He praised Allah, and then he said:
"Whoever among you worshipped Muhammad, then Muhammad has died.
And whoever worshipped Allah, then Allah is living, and does not die." <!-- c36-008 -->

And he recited the verse that had been revealed after Uhud.
"Muhammad is no more than a messenger. Messengers have passed away before him.
If he dies, or is killed, will you turn back on your heels?" <!-- c36-009 -->

His Companion Ibn Abbas said: "By Allah, it was as though the people had not known that Allah had revealed this verse, until Abu Bakr recited it.
Then all the people took it from him, and there was not a man I heard who was not reciting it." <!-- c36-008 -->

And Umar said: "By Allah, when I heard Abu Bakr recite it, my legs would not hold me.
I fell to the ground.
And I knew that the Prophet had died." <!-- c36-008 -->

## s05 -- The Burial
Image: desert/dunes-night.svg | slow-zoom | Desert at night under a thin moon. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Aishah said that when they came to wash him, they did not know whether to remove his garments, as they did with their dead.
In the end they washed him in his garment, pouring the water over it. <!-- c36-010 -->

The historians of the Seerah report that the people came in to pray over him in groups, one group after another, with no one leading them. <!-- c36-011 -->

And he was buried where he had died: in the room of Aishah, beside the mosque he had built. <!-- c36-012 -->

Anas, who had been a boy when he arrived in Madinah, said:
"On the day the Messenger of Allah entered Madinah, everything in it was filled with light.
And on the day he died, everything in it grew dark." <!-- c36-013 -->

When the burial was done, Fatimah said to him:
"Anas, how could your hearts bear to pour earth over the Messenger of Allah?" <!-- c36-005 -->
