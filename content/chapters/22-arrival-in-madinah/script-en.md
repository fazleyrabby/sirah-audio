# Arrival in Madinah

## s01 -- Waiting at the Lava Field
Image: desert/dunes-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | Empty desert at dawn, light spreading over the dunes. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

In Yathrib, the believers had heard that he had left Makkah. <!-- c22-001 -->

Each morning they went out to the edge of the lava field, south of the town, and watched the road.
They waited until the heat of midday drove them back to their houses. <!-- c22-001 -->

One day they had waited a long time, and gone home.
A Jewish man of the town climbed to the top of one of their forts, to look out for something of his own.
And he saw them: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and his companions, in white, coming out of the shimmer of the desert. <!-- c22-001 -->

He could not keep himself from shouting at the top of his voice:
"O Arabs! Here is your great one, whom you have been waiting for!" <!-- c22-001 -->

The Muslims ran for their weapons, and went out to meet him at the head of the lava field. <!-- c22-001 -->

## s02 -- Quba
Image: desert/oasis-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | Palm trees at an oasis at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

He turned aside with them to the right, and stopped among the clan of Amr ibn Awf, in the village of Quba.
It was a Monday, in the month of Rabi' al-Awwal. <!-- c22-002 -->

Abu Bakr stood to receive the people.
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ sat in silence.
Those of the Ansar who had never seen him came forward and greeted Abu Bakr. <!-- c22-002 -->

Then the sun reached the place where the Messenger of Allah was sitting.
Abu Bakr came, and shaded him with his cloak.
And then the people knew which of the two he was. <!-- c22-002 -->

He stayed in Quba for some days.
And there he founded a mosque: the one of which the Qur'an says that it was founded on piety from its first day. <!-- c22-003 -->

## s03 -- The Prophet of Allah Has Come
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a town among mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Then he sent for his mother's kinsmen, the clan of an-Najjar.
They came wearing their swords, and he rode on, with Abu Bakr behind him, and the men of the Ansar around him. <!-- c22-004 -->

Anas, who was a boy then, said that word went through the town.
People came up onto the roofs and out into the lanes, looking for him, and saying:
"The Prophet of Allah has come! The Prophet of Allah has come!" <!-- c22-005 -->

Al-Bara said: "I never saw the people of Madinah rejoice at anything as they rejoiced at the coming of the Messenger of Allah." <!-- c22-006 -->

A well-known song of welcome is said to have been sung to him that day.
The report of it is not established, and so we do not relate it. <!-- c22-007 -->

## s04 -- Where the Camel Knelt
Image: desert/dunes-day.svg | pan-right | Open ground under a pale sky. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

He rode on, with the people walking beside him, until his camel knelt. <!-- c22-008 -->

The place was an open yard used for drying dates.
It belonged to two orphan boys, Sahl and Suhayl, who were in the care of As'ad ibn Zurarah. <!-- c22-008 -->

When the camel knelt, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "This, if Allah wills, is the place." <!-- c22-008 -->

He called the two boys, and asked them to name a price for the yard, so that he could make it a mosque.
They said: "No. We give it to you, Messenger of Allah."
He would not accept it as a gift from them.
He bought it. <!-- c22-008 -->

The nearest house belonged to a man named Abu Ayyub.
He said: "This is my house, and this is my door."
And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ stayed with him. <!-- c22-009 -->

## s05 -- The First Words
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-left | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Among those who came to look at him that day was a learned man of the Jews of the town, Abdullah ibn Salam. <!-- c22-010 -->

He said: "When the Messenger of Allah arrived, the people hurried out to him.
I went among them, to look at him.
And when I saw his face clearly, I knew that it was not the face of a liar." <!-- c22-010 -->

"The first thing I heard him say was this:
O people. Spread peace.
Feed others.
And pray at night, while people are sleeping.
You will enter Paradise in peace." <!-- c22-010 -->

## s06 -- A New Name
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

From then on, Yathrib had another name: Madinah, the City. <!-- c22-011 -->

Years later, when his Companions needed a starting point from which to count their years, they did not choose the year the revelation began, nor the year of his death.
They chose the year he arrived in Madinah. <!-- c22-012 -->

The Makkan years were over.
The work of building a community had begun. <!-- n -->
