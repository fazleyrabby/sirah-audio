# The Beginning of Prophethood

## s01 -- A Task Given
Image: desert/dunes-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | Empty desert at dawn, light spreading over the dunes. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

"Arise and warn."
With those words, the man who had come down from Hira was given a task. <!-- c08-001 -->

The verses that followed told him what that task would require of him.
Their meaning is:
"Magnify your Lord.
Purify your garments.
Keep away from all defilement.
Do not give in order to gain more.
And for the sake of your Lord, be patient." <!-- c08-001 -->

From that time, the narration tells us, the revelation came strongly, and without interruption. <!-- c08-002 -->

## s02 -- How Revelation Came
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

One of his Companions, al-Harith ibn Hisham, once asked him how the revelation came to him. <!-- c08-003 -->

He said: "Sometimes it comes to me like the ringing of a bell, and that is the hardest of it upon me.
Then it leaves me, and I have grasped what was said.
And sometimes the angel comes to me in the form of a man, and speaks to me, and I grasp what he says." <!-- c08-003 -->

His wife Aishah said that she saw him when revelation came down on a day of severe cold.
When it left him, his forehead was running with sweat. <!-- c08-003 -->

## s03 -- Learning the Words
Image: mountains/hira-night.svg | pan-right | A bare mountain under a night sky with a thin moon. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

In the beginning, he strained to hold on to what was being revealed.
His Companion Ibn Abbas said that he would move his lips with the words as they came, afraid that he might lose them. <!-- c08-004 -->

Then these verses were revealed.
Their meaning is:
"Do not move your tongue with it, to hasten it.
It is for Us to gather it, and to have it recited.
So when We have recited it, follow its recitation.
Then it is for Us to make it clear." <!-- c08-005 -->

After that, Ibn Abbas said, he would listen when Jibril came.
And when Jibril had gone, he would recite it as it had been recited to him. <!-- c08-004 -->

## s04 -- The Night Prayer
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | pan-left | A valley town at night beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Among the early revelations was a command to stand in prayer at night.
Its meaning is:
"O you who are wrapped up.
Stand through the night, except for a little.
Half of it, or a little less,
or a little more.
And recite the Qur'an in a measured recitation.
We are going to cast upon you a weighty word." <!-- c08-006 -->

## s05 -- The Quiet Call
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-right | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

He did not begin by standing in the marketplace.
The historians of the Seerah report that he began with those nearest to him, and with those he trusted,
and that for about three years the call was carried from one person to another, in private. <!-- c08-007 -->

They report that when the believers wished to pray, they would go out to the ravines around Makkah, away from the eyes of their people. <!-- c08-008 -->

And they report that the believers came to gather in the house of a young man named al-Arqam, near the hill of Safa,
where the Messenger of Allah ﷺ would teach them what had been revealed. <!-- c08-009 -->

## s06 -- The Message
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a valley town ringed by mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

A man named Amr ibn Abasah came to him in those days, when he was still keeping out of sight of his people.
Amr asked him: "What are you?"
He said: "I am a prophet."
Amr asked: "And what is a prophet?"
He said: "Allah has sent me."
Amr asked: "With what has He sent you?"
He said: "He has sent me to join the ties of kinship, to break the idols,
and so that Allah alone is worshipped, with nothing set beside Him." <!-- c08-010 -->

That was the message, in a single breath.
Who answered it first is where we turn next. <!-- n -->
