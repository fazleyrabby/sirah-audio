# The Night of Hijrah

## s01 -- At Noon
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | A valley town ringed by mountains in strong light. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Aishah was in her father's house on the day it began.
She narrated what she saw. <!-- c19-001 -->

"We were sitting in the house of Abu Bakr in the heat of midday," she said,
"when someone said to him: Here is the Messenger of Allah, with his head covered, coming at an hour when he never comes to us." <!-- c19-001 -->

Abu Bakr said: "May my father and mother be his ransom.
By Allah, nothing has brought him at this hour but something grave." <!-- c19-001 -->

He asked leave to enter, and came in.
He said to Abu Bakr: "Send out those who are with you."
Abu Bakr said: "They are only your own family, Messenger of Allah."
He said: "I have been given permission to leave." <!-- c19-001 -->

Abu Bakr said: "To be your companion, Messenger of Allah?"
He said: "Yes." <!-- c19-001 -->

## s02 -- Two Camels
Image: desert/dunes-day.svg | pan-left | Empty desert dunes under a pale sky. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Abu Bakr said: "Then take one of these two camels of mine."
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "For its price." <!-- c19-002 -->

Aishah said: "We made them ready as quickly as we could.
We put food for them in a leather bag.
My sister Asma cut a strip from her waistband, and tied the mouth of the bag with it."
And for that, she said, Asma was called: She of the Two Waistbands. <!-- c19-002 -->

They had hired a guide, a man of the tribe of ad-Dil, who knew the roads well.
He followed the religion of Quraysh.
But they trusted him.
They handed their two camels over to him, and told him to bring them, after three nights, to the cave on Mount Thawr. <!-- c19-003 -->

## s03 -- The House Surrounded
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-right | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The historians of the Seerah report what happened that night at his own house. <!-- c19-004 -->

They report that the young men chosen by Quraysh gathered at his door after dark, waiting for him to come out. <!-- c19-004 -->

They report that he asked Ali to sleep in his bed that night, covered with his green cloak,
and to stay behind in Makkah until he had returned to their owners the things that people had left in his keeping. <!-- c19-005 -->

For even then, the people of Makkah who would not accept his message still entrusted him with their valuables. <!-- c19-005 -->

And the historians report that he walked out past the men at his door, and they did not see him.
He was reciting from the Qur'an.
The meaning of the verse is:
"We have set a barrier before them, and a barrier behind them, and We have covered them, so that they do not see." <!-- c19-006 -->

## s04 -- Leaving Makkah
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | slow-zoom | A valley town at night beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

He was leaving the town where he had been born, and where he had spent most of his fifty-three years. <!-- c19-007 -->

## s05 -- South
Image: mountains/hira-night.svg | pan-left | A bare mountain under a night sky with a thin moon. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Yathrib lay to the north.
He and Abu Bakr went south. <!-- c19-009 -->

Aishah said: "Then the Messenger of Allah and Abu Bakr reached a cave in the mountain of Thawr.
And they stayed hidden in it for three nights." <!-- c19-010 -->

Behind them, the historians report, the men at his door waited until dawn.
When they went in, they found Ali. <!-- c19-004 -->
