# The Trench

## s01 -- The Confederates
Image: maps/arabia.svg | static | A map of the Arabian peninsula with Makkah and Madinah marked. | 0 | 24% 50%
Audio: Voice only.

Two years after Uhud, Quraysh came a third time.
This time they did not come alone. <!-- c27-001 -->

The historians of the Seerah report that they gathered the tribes of Arabia into a single force, some ten thousand strong, and marched on Madinah.
It was the fifth year after the Hijrah.
The Qur'an calls them al-Ahzab: the Confederates. <!-- c27-001 -->

Madinah could not meet such an army in the open.
The historians report that Salman, a Companion who had come from Persia, suggested something the Arabs had not used before:
a trench, dug across the open northern side of the town. <!-- c27-002 -->

## s02 -- Digging
Image: desert/dunes-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | Bare ground in the cold light of early morning. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

They dug it themselves. <!-- c27-003 -->

Anas said that the Emigrants and the Ansar dug on cold mornings, carrying the earth on their backs.
They had no servants to do it for them. <!-- c27-003 -->
And as they worked, they chanted:
"We are those who have pledged to Muhammad,
to hold to Islam for as long as we live." <!-- c27-003 -->

And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ would answer them:
"O Allah, there is no good but the good of the Hereafter.
So bless the Ansar, and the Emigrants." <!-- c27-003 -->

He dug with them.
Al-Bara said: "I saw him carrying earth, until the dust hid the skin of his stomach."
And he heard him reciting, as he carried it, the lines of a poet among his Companions:
"By Allah, had it not been for Allah, we would not have been guided.
We would not have given charity, and we would not have prayed.
So send down calm upon us,
and make our feet firm when we meet them." <!-- c27-004 -->

## s03 -- Hunger
Image: desert/dunes-night.svg | slow-zoom | Desert at night under a thin moon. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

There was little to eat. <!-- c27-005 -->

Jabir said that as they dug, they struck a great rock that would not break.
They went to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.
He said: "I will come down."
He rose, Jabir said, with a stone bound against his stomach.
For they had gone three days without tasting food. <!-- c27-005 -->

He took the pick, and struck the rock.
And it crumbled into sand. <!-- c27-005 -->

## s04 -- The Siege
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | pan-right | A town at night beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The Confederates arrived, and found the trench between them and the town.
They made their camp beyond it. <!-- c27-006 -->

The siege lasted, the historians report, for close to a month. <!-- c27-006 -->

During it, they report, the Jewish tribe of Qurayzah, who lived within Madinah and were bound to it by treaty, broke that treaty and sided with the besiegers.
The Muslims now had an enemy before them, and an enemy behind. <!-- c27-007 -->

The Qur'an describes those days.
Its meaning is:
"When they came at you from above you, and from below you.
When eyes swerved, and hearts rose to the throats.
There the believers were tested, and shaken with a mighty shaking." <!-- c27-008 -->

And it describes what the believers said:
"This is what Allah and His Messenger promised us.
Allah and His Messenger spoke the truth.
And it only increased them in faith, and in submission." <!-- c27-009 -->

## s05 -- The Wind
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ called upon his Lord against the besiegers:
"O Allah, who sent down the Book, who is swift in reckoning: defeat the Confederates.
O Allah, defeat them, and shake them." <!-- c27-010 -->

Then came a night of bitter cold, and a violent wind. <!-- c27-011 -->

His Companion Hudhayfah told of it.
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ asked who would bring him news of the enemy.
No one answered, for the cold.
So he called Hudhayfah by name, and sent him, telling him not to provoke them. <!-- c27-011 -->

Hudhayfah went into their camp.
He found the wind tearing at their tents, and overturning their pots.
And he returned to say that they were leaving. <!-- c27-011 -->

The Qur'an says:
"Remember the favour of Allah upon you, when armies came against you,
and We sent against them a wind, and forces you did not see." <!-- c27-012 -->

## s06 -- They Will Not Come Again
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a town among mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

In the morning, the plain beyond the trench was empty. <!-- c27-012 c27-013 -->

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:
"Now we shall go to them. They will not come to us." <!-- c27-013 -->

Then he turned to the tribe of Qurayzah, who had broken their treaty in the hour of danger.
They were besieged, and agreed to accept the judgement of Sa'd ibn Mu'adh, a leader of the Ansar who had been their ally.
And Sa'd judged between them. <!-- c27-014 -->

Quraysh had brought against Madinah the largest army Arabia had seen.
They never marched on it again. <!-- c27-013 -->
