# The Community Order

## s01 -- A Town of Many Peoples
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a town among mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Madinah was not one people. <!-- c24-001 -->

There were the Emigrants from Makkah.
There were the two tribes of the Ansar, the Aws and the Khazraj, who had been at war with each other only a few years before.
There were those among the Arabs of the town who had not accepted Islam.
And there were the Jewish tribes, with their own forts, their own scholars, and their own law. <!-- c24-001 -->

His Companion Usamah described a gathering he passed with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ in those first months.
In that one gathering, he said, there were Muslims, idol-worshippers, and Jews, sitting together. <!-- c24-002 -->

## s02 -- The Document
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-right | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The historians of the Seerah report that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ had a document written, setting out how these groups would live together.
It is often called the Constitution of Madinah. <!-- c24-003 -->

The full text comes to us through the historians, without a complete chain of narration.
So we give its main terms as they report them. <!-- c24-003 -->

They report that it declared the believers, Emigrants and Ansar, to be one community, apart from other people.
Each clan would remain responsible for its own blood-money, and for ransoming its own captives.
And the believers would stand together against anyone among them who did wrong, even if he were the son of one of them. <!-- c24-004 -->

## s03 -- The Jewish Tribes
Image: desert/oasis-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | Palm trees at an oasis at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

They report that the document also set terms with the Jewish tribes of the town. <!-- c24-005 -->

The Jews would have their religion, and the Muslims theirs.
Each side would bear its own expenses.
They would help one another against anyone who attacked the people of this document.
And between them there would be sincere counsel, and good faith, and no treachery. <!-- c24-005 -->

Madinah itself, the document said, would be a sanctuary for all who were party to it.
And whatever dispute arose among them that might lead to harm would be referred to Allah, and to Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah. <!-- c24-006 -->

## s04 -- What the Hadith Confirm
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Parts of this are confirmed in the most trusted collections of hadith. <!-- c24-007 c24-008 -->

Anas said: "The Prophet made the alliance between Quraysh and the Ansar in my house." <!-- c24-007 -->

And Ali said that he had a written sheet from the Prophet.
In it was written that Madinah is a sanctuary.
And in it was written:
"The protection given by the Muslims is one.
The least of them may give it on behalf of all.
And whoever betrays a Muslim in it, upon him is the curse of Allah, and the angels, and all the people." <!-- c24-008 -->

Any one believer, however humble, could give his word to a stranger.
And every other believer was bound to honour it. <!-- c24-008 -->

## s05 -- The Man Who Would Be King
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | pan-left | A valley town at night beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Not everyone in Madinah was glad of the new order. <!-- c24-009 -->

In that gathering Usamah described, there was a man named Abdullah ibn Ubayy.
When the dust of the Prophet's mount drifted over them, he covered his nose with his cloak, and said: "Do not raise dust over us."
And when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ greeted them and recited the Qur'an to them, he said:
"Man, there is nothing better than what you say, if it is true.
So do not trouble us with it in our gatherings.
Go back to your mount, and tell it to whoever comes to you." <!-- c24-009 -->

The Messenger of Allah went on to visit Sa'd ibn Ubadah, a leader of the Khazraj, and told him what had been said.
Sa'd said: "Pardon him, Messenger of Allah, and overlook it.
The people of this town had agreed to crown him.
When Allah prevented that through the truth He gave you, it choked him." <!-- c24-009 -->

And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ pardoned him. <!-- c24-009 -->

## s06 -- Permission to Fight
Image: desert/dunes-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | Empty desert at dawn, light spreading over the dunes. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

For thirteen years in Makkah, the believers had been told to endure, and to hold back their hands.
Now they had a home, and Quraysh had not given up. <!-- c24-010 -->

Then a verse was revealed.
Its meaning is:
"Permission is given to those who are fought against, because they have been wronged.
And Allah is able to give them victory.
Those who were driven from their homes without right, only because they said: Our Lord is Allah." <!-- c24-011 -->
