# Hunayn

## s01 -- After Makkah
Image: mountains/ridges-dusk.svg | pan-left | Layered mountain ridges at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

After the conquest of Makkah, Hawazin and Thaqif gathered to fight. <!-- c32-001 -->

East of Makkah lived the great tribe of Hawazin, and with them Thaqif, the people of Ta'if.
The historians of the Seerah report that when they heard that Makkah had fallen, they gathered to strike before they were struck. <!-- c32-001 -->

Anas said that they came out with their flocks, and their women and children, to fight. <!-- c32-002 -->

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ went out to meet them.
With him were the ten thousand who had come from Madinah, and people of Makkah who had accepted Islam only days before. <!-- c32-002 -->

## s02 -- Your Great Numbers
Image: desert/dunes-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | A valley floor in the grey light before sunrise. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

It was the largest force the Muslims had ever fielded.
And the Qur'an tells us what some of them felt.
Its meaning is:
"On the day of Hunayn, when your great numbers pleased you.
But they availed you nothing.
And the earth, for all its breadth, became narrow for you.
Then you turned back in retreat." <!-- c32-003 -->

The historians report that the army entered the valley of Hunayn in the half-light before dawn,
and that the archers of Hawazin were already waiting on the slopes above. <!-- c32-004 -->

Al-Bara said: "The people of Hawazin were archers.
They met them with a volley of arrows." <!-- c32-005 -->
The front of the army broke, and men fled past the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. <!-- c32-005 -->

## s03 -- I Am the Prophet
Image: desert/dunes-day.svg | pan-right | Open ground under a pale sky. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Years afterwards, a man asked al-Bara: "Did you flee on the day of Hunayn?" <!-- c32-005 -->

Al-Bara said: "I bear witness that the Prophet of Allah did not turn back.
He was on his white mule, and his cousin Abu Sufyan ibn al-Harith was holding its bridle.
And he was saying:
I am the Prophet, and that is no lie.
I am the son of Abd al-Muttalib." <!-- c32-005 -->

Anas said that he called to his right: "O company of the Ansar!"
They answered: "We are here, Messenger of Allah. Be glad. We are with you."
He called to his left: "O company of the Ansar!"
And they answered the same. <!-- c32-002 -->

The Qur'an says what followed.
"Then Allah sent down His calm upon His Messenger, and upon the believers.
And He sent down forces that you did not see." <!-- c32-006 -->

They came back to him, and the day turned. <!-- c32-002 -->

## s04 -- The Spoils
Image: desert/oasis-dusk.svg | slow-zoom | Palm trees at an oasis at dusk. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Hawazin came with their families and animals. After the battle, many of their families were taken captive. <!-- c32-002 c32-009 -->

When the Messenger of Allah ﷺ came to divide the spoils, he gave great gifts to the leaders of Quraysh, the men who had only just entered Islam.
To the Ansar, he gave nothing. <!-- c32-007 -->

Some of the Ansar were hurt by it.
It reached him, and he gathered them together, alone, and spoke to them. <!-- c32-007 -->

"O company of the Ansar.
Did I not find you astray, and Allah guided you through me?
Did I not find you divided, and Allah joined your hearts through me?
Did I not find you poor, and Allah enriched you through me?"
To each question they answered: "Allah and His Messenger have been most generous." <!-- c32-007 -->

## s05 -- You Go Home with the Messenger of Allah
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | slow-zoom | A valley at night beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Then he said:
"Are you not content that the people should go away with sheep and camels,
and that you should go back to your homes with the Prophet?" <!-- c32-007 -->

"Had it not been for the Hijrah, I would have been a man of the Ansar.
If all the people took one valley, and the Ansar took another, I would take the valley of the Ansar.
The Ansar are the garment next to my skin.
And the rest of the people are the outer garment." <!-- c32-007 -->



Makkah was his birthplace, and it was now a Muslim city.
He did not stay there.
He went home with the Ansar, to Madinah. <!-- c32-008 -->

## s06 -- The Captives of Hawazin
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Before he left, a delegation of Hawazin came to him.
They had accepted Islam, and they asked for the return of their families and their property. <!-- c32-009 -->

He said: "The speech I love best is the truest.
Choose one of the two: your families, or your wealth."
They chose their families. <!-- c32-009 -->

He stood before the Muslims, and said that these brothers of theirs had come repentant, and that he thought it right to return their families to them.
Whoever wished to give up his share freely, let him do so.
And the people said: "We give it freely, Messenger of Allah." <!-- c32-009 -->

The women and children of Hawazin went home. <!-- c32-009 -->
