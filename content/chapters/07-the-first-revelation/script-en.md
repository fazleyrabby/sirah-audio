## s01 -- The Mountain
Image: mountains/hira-night.svg | slow-zoom | A bare mountain under a night sky with a thin moon. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Above the valley of Makkah rises a mountain of bare, dark rock.
Near its summit there is a small cave, high above the town.
The cave is called Hira. <!-- c07-001 -->

It was to this cave that Muhammad ﷺ would withdraw, in the time before he was called to prophethood.
He would stay there in worship for a number of nights,
then return to his family, gather provisions, and go back again. <!-- c07-003 -->

## s02 -- True Dreams
Image: makkah/valley-night.svg | pan-left | A valley town at night beneath the stars. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Our account of what happened there comes from his wife Aishah, may Allah be pleased with her,
in a narration preserved in the most trusted collections of hadith. <!-- c07-002 -->

She said that revelation first came to the Messenger of Allah in the form of true dreams in his sleep.
Whatever he saw in a dream came true, as clearly as the breaking of dawn. <!-- c07-004 -->

After this, she said, solitude was made beloved to him.
And so he went up to Hira. <!-- c07-003 -->

He was, by then, about forty years old. <!-- c07-005 -->

The Qur'an tells us that it was sent down in the month of Ramadan, on a night it calls the Night of Decree. <!-- c07-006 -->
The exact date of that night has been reported differently, and we do not fix one here. <!-- c07-007 -->

## s03 -- Read
Image: desert/night-sky.svg | slow-zoom | A wide night sky full of stars above a dark ridge. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Then, in the cave of Hira, the truth came to him. <!-- c07-008 -->

The angel came and said: "Read."
He answered: "I am not one who reads." <!-- c07-008 -->

He later described what followed.
The angel took hold of him, and pressed him until he could bear it no more, then released him and said again: "Read."
Again he answered: "I am not one who reads."
A second time the angel pressed him, and released him, and said: "Read."
A third time he gave the same answer, and a third time he was pressed and released. <!-- c07-008 -->

Then the angel recited the words that were the first of the Qur'an to be revealed.
Their meaning is: <!-- c07-009 -->

"Read, in the name of your Lord who created.
Created man from a clinging clot.
Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous.
Who taught by the pen.
Taught man what he did not know." <!-- c07-009 -->

The first word was a command to read,
given to a man who, as the Qur'an itself says, had never recited a scripture before it, nor written one with his hand. <!-- c07-018 -->

## s04 -- The Return
Image: makkah/valley-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | First light over a valley town ringed by mountains. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ returned with those words, his heart trembling.
He came to his wife Khadijah, may Allah be pleased with her, and said: "Cover me. Cover me."
They covered him until the fear had left him. <!-- c07-010 -->

Then he told her what had happened, and said: "I feared for myself." <!-- c07-010 -->

Khadijah answered him:
"Never. By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you.
You keep the ties of kinship.
You carry the burdens of the weak.
You give to those who have nothing.
You honour your guest.
And you help those who are struck by hardship." <!-- c07-011 -->

She did not answer with reassurance alone.
She answered by describing the character she knew. <!-- n -->

## s05 -- Waraqah
Image: makkah/homes-night.svg | pan-right | Flat-roofed houses at night with a few lamp-lit windows. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

Khadijah then took him to her cousin, Waraqah ibn Nawfal.
Waraqah had become a Christian in the days before Islam.
He knew the earlier scriptures, and used to write from the Gospel.
He was by then an old man, and had lost his sight. <!-- c07-012 -->

Khadijah said to him: "Cousin, listen to your nephew."
Waraqah asked: "Nephew, what have you seen?"
And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ told him. <!-- c07-012 -->

Waraqah said: "This is the Namus that Allah sent down to Musa."
By the Namus, he meant the angel who brings revelation.
"I wish I were young," he said.
"I wish I could be alive when your people drive you out." <!-- c07-013 -->

The Messenger of Allah asked: "Will they drive me out?" <!-- c07-013 -->

"Yes," said Waraqah.
"No man has ever come with the like of what you have brought without being met with hostility.
If I live to see that day, I will support you with all my strength." <!-- c07-013 -->

But Waraqah did not live long after this.
He died, and for a time the revelation paused. <!-- c07-014 -->

## s06 -- Arise and Warn
Image: desert/dunes-dawn.svg | slow-zoom | Empty desert at dawn, light spreading over the dunes. No figures.
Audio: Voice only.

How long that pause lasted is reported differently, and we do not fix a number. <!-- c07-015 -->

What ended it was narrated by the Companion Jabir ibn Abdullah, who heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ describe it.
He was walking when he heard a voice from the sky.
He looked up, and there was the angel who had come to him at Hira, seated between the heaven and the earth.
He was struck with awe, and returned home, saying: "Wrap me up. Wrap me up." <!-- c07-016 -->

Then these verses were revealed.
Their meaning is: <!-- c07-017 -->

"O you who are wrapped in your cloak.
Arise and warn.
Magnify your Lord.
Purify your garments.
And keep away from all defilement." <!-- c07-017 -->

After that, the narration says, the revelation came strongly and without interruption. <!-- c07-016 -->

The time of solitude on the mountain was over.
The time of the message had begun. <!-- n -->
